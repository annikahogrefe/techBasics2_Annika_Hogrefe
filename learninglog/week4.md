### Notes
(also avalible on my [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/Week-4-Nov-3-Buttons-Buzzers-40d1a46c8a6e80bc9661e52709106009?source=copy_link))

- Buzzers:
    - Active Buzzer: it can produce sound directly when
    connected to power (built in oscillator, defined pitch), longer leg is +
    - Passive Buzzer: it requires a signal in order to make sound
      
- Standard soundwave for Ardurino/Electronics: Pulse (however others such as Sinewave are also possible using fade in/fade out)

- Ultrasonic Distance Sensor:
    - read legs to know what to connect
        
- Buttons:
    - place on the spine/middle line
    - pushing connects the top and bottom line
