- Motor Comparison:

|  | **Servos** | **Stepper Motors** | **DC Motors** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Control System | No feedback | High precision | Precise control |
| Speed & Torque | High torque at low speed | Good at dynamic tasks with high speed changes | High speed |
| Applications | Low-speed, high precision tasks | Dynamsic, high-performance tasks | High-speed applications |

- **Diode:**
    - We put a diode between the and the - of a DC motor to protect the control electronics from voltage spikes generated by the motor
    - stripe to the gng
  
- **Transistor:**
    - A semiconductor device used to switch or amplify electrical signals and power
    - Used as a digital switch eg for a DC Motor
    - NPN/PNP: direction of current flow is different
    - Collector (C) collects the majority of the charge carriers that are emitted from the emitter
    - Base (B)This is the control terminal. A small current or voltage at the base can control a larger current flow between the collector and emitter → Signal from Ardurino
    - Emitter (E) emits the charge carriers (electrons in an NPN transistor) into the base → connect to GND
    - 
- **DC Motor Controllers:**
    - Dual H-Bridge motor controller
    - Popular controllers: L293D, L298N, TB6612fng, DRV8833
    - Usually you need to drive them with a stable power supply /LiPo batter y! (9V batter y doesn't work so well)
      
- **MOSFET:** Switch that can handle more power than a transistor
- **Relay:** isolates the control circuit from the load for safety
- **Switches:**
- |  | **Transistors** | **Mosefets** | **Relays** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism | current controlled | voltage controlled | electromechanic |
| Speed & Torque | High torque at low speed | Good at dynamic tasks with high speed changes | High speed |
| Applications | Low-speed, high precision tasks | Dynamsic, high-performance tasks | High-speed applications |
